# Bibliografía

## Quinasas y oncología

- Manning, G. et al. (2002). *The protein kinase complement of the human genome*.
  **Science** 298:1912–1934. — El artículo que define el quinoma humano.
- Cohen, P., Cross, D. & Jänne, P. A. (2021). *Kinase drug discovery 20 years after
  imatinib*. **Nature Reviews Drug Discovery** 20:551–569.
- Roskoski, R. (actualización anual). *Properties of FDA-approved small molecule protein
  kinase inhibitors*. **Pharmacological Research**. — Catálogo actualizado de aprobados.
- Attwood, M. M. et al. (2021). *Trends in kinase drug discovery: targets, indications and
  inhibitor design*. **Nature Reviews Drug Discovery** 20:839–861.

## Datos de bioactividad

- Zdrazil, B. et al. (2024). *The ChEMBL Database in 2023*. **Nucleic Acids Research**
  52:D1180–D1192.
- Davis, M. I. et al. (2011). *Comprehensive analysis of kinase inhibitor selectivity*.
  **Nature Biotechnology** 29:1046–1051. — Matriz de 72 inhibidores × 442 quinasas.
- Metz, J. T. et al. (2011). *Navigating the kinome*. **Nature Chemical Biology**
  7:200–202.
- Drewry, D. H. et al. (2017). *Progress towards a public chemogenomic set for protein
  kinases (PKIS)*. **PLOS ONE** 12:e0181585.

## Metodología QSAR

- Rogers, D. & Hahn, M. (2010). *Extended-connectivity fingerprints*. **Journal of
  Chemical Information and Modeling** 50:742–754. — Define ECFP/Morgan.
- Bemis, G. W. & Murcko, M. A. (1996). *The properties of known drugs. 1. Molecular
  frameworks*. **Journal of Medicinal Chemistry** 39:2887–2893. — Define el scaffold que
  se usa en la partición.
- Sheridan, R. P. (2013). *Time-split cross-validation as a method for estimating the
  goodness of prospective prediction*. **Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling**
  53:783–790. — Por qué la validación aleatoria engaña.
- Wu, Z. et al. (2018). *MoleculeNet: a benchmark for molecular machine learning*.
  **Chemical Science** 9:513–530.
- Yang, K. et al. (2019). *Analyzing learned molecular representations for property
  prediction*. **Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling** 59:3370–3388. —
  ChemProp / D-MPNN.
- Tropsha, A. (2010). *Best practices for QSAR model development, validation, and
  exploitation*. **Molecular Informatics** 29:476–488.
- Sahigara, F. et al. (2012). *Comparison of different approaches to define the
  applicability domain of QSAR models*. **Molecules** 17:4791–4810.

## Interpretabilidad

- Lundberg, S. M. & Lee, S.-I. (2017). *A unified approach to interpreting model
  predictions*. **NeurIPS 30**. — SHAP.
- Lundberg, S. M. et al. (2020). *From local explanations to global understanding with
  explainable AI for trees*. **Nature Machine Intelligence** 2:56–67.
- Jiménez-Luna, J., Grisoni, F. & Schneider, G. (2020). *Drug discovery with explainable
  artificial intelligence*. **Nature Machine Intelligence** 2:573–584.
